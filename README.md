# Awesome-Fashion-Product-Lifecycle-Management

## Top Fashion Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Apparel & Footwear Product Development, Tech Packs, BOM, Sourcing, Collections & Seasonal Workflows*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Fashion Product Lifecycle Management (PLM)**. These systems help apparel, footwear, and lifestyle brands manage product data from concept through design, development, sampling, sourcing, and production—tech packs, BOMs, colorways, materials, and seasonal calendars.



**Examples** include Centric PLM, PTC FlexPLM, Lectra Kubix Link, Gerber YuniquePLM, DeSL PLM, CGS BlueCherry PLM, Aptean Apparel PLM, Backbone PLM, WFX PLM, and Visual PLM (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Fashion-specific PLM is almost entirely commercial. General open PLM systems (**Dokuly**, **Cascadia PLM**, **GitPLM**) and apparel modules inside open ERPs (**Odoo**, **ERPNext**) provide partial alternatives. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the specialized commercial gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Centric PLM](https://www.centricsoftware.com/)**  

  Leading fashion, apparel, footwear, and lifestyle PLM platform used by mid-market to enterprise brands for end-to-end product development, sourcing, and collection management.



- **[PTC FlexPLM](https://www.ptc.com/)**  

  Enterprise PLM solution with strong fashion and retail industry configurations for product data, collaboration, and lifecycle processes.



- **[Lectra Kubix Link](https://www.lectra.com/)**  

  Fashion PLM and collaboration platform tightly integrated with Lectra’s design and cutting ecosystem—visual planning, product data, and collection development.



- **[Gerber YuniquePLM](https://www.gerbertechnology.com/)**  

  Collaborative PLM for apparel product development, enabling teams to manage styles, tech packs, and virtual collaboration across locations.



- **[DeSL PLM](https://www.desl.com/)**  

  Fashion and apparel PLM focused on product development, sourcing, and supply-chain collaboration for brands and manufacturers.



- **[CGS BlueCherry PLM](https://www.cgsinc.com/)**  

  PLM capabilities within the BlueCherry suite, often paired with ERP for apparel and fashion businesses.



- **[Aptean Apparel PLM](https://www.aptean.com/)**  

  Apparel-focused PLM and related solutions for product lifecycle, manufacturing, and supply-chain processes.



- **[Backbone PLM](https://www.backboneplm.com/)**  

  Cloud-native, more affordable PLM aimed at startups and smaller fashion brands for product data and development workflows.



- **[WFX PLM](https://www.worldfashionexchange.com/)**  

  Mid-market fashion PLM covering planning, design, development, and sourcing with emphasis on ease of use and faster implementation.



- **[Visual PLM and related fashion PLM tools](https://www.example.com/)**  

  Additional platforms focused on visual product development, tech-pack management, and seasonal collection workflows.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Dokuly](https://github.com/Dokuly-PLM/dokuly)**  

  Open-source Product Lifecycle Management system with part numbering, BOM management, revision control, documents, inventory, and release workflows—usable as a general PLM foundation.



- **[Cascadia PLM](https://github.com/cascadia-plm)**  

  Open-source, code-first PLM (AGPL) with a modern web UI, unified item model, workflows, and AI-assisted features aimed at engineering and manufacturing teams.



- **[GitPLM](https://github.com/git-plm)**  

  Git-based product lifecycle approach—parts, BOMs, and releases managed as plain-text files alongside design data with full history and review workflows.



- **[PLMore and other community open PLM efforts](https://github.com/PLMore/PLMore)**  

  Emerging open-source PLM projects seeking lighter, modern alternatives to traditional enterprise PLM systems.



- **[Odoo Apparel / manufacturing community modules](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open ERP modules that can support product data, BOMs, variants, and basic product-development processes for fashion and apparel.



- **[ERPNext manufacturing and product data features](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open-source ERP capabilities for items, BOMs, variants, and production planning that smaller brands sometimes adapt for product lifecycle needs.



- **[BOM and tech-pack open document templates](https://github.com/)**  

  Community templates and structured data formats for style sheets, material lists, and construction details.



- **[Material and color library open managers](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple open tools for organizing fabrics, trims, colors, and supplier references.



- **[Version control and collaboration open patterns](https://github.com/)**  

  Using Git, wiki, or document platforms to maintain product data and change history for small collections.



- **[Documentation and fashion product-development open playbooks](https://github.com/)**  

  Guides for adapting general open PLM or ERP systems to apparel workflows.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **Dokuly**, **Cascadia**, or **GitPLM** as a general product-data backbone when a full commercial fashion PLM is not yet justified.

- Configuring **Odoo** or **ERPNext** for styles, variants, BOMs, and supplier collaboration in smaller brands.

- Accepting that fashion-specific features (seasonal calendars, visual line boards, tech-pack generation, material libraries, multi-brand/multi-season complexity, and CAD integrations) still strongly favor commercial platforms (Centric, Lectra, PTC FlexPLM, WFX, Backbone, Gerber Yunique, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, BOM transparency, and lower cost for early-stage or very small brands.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Maintain product master data and BOMs in an open PLM or ERP → manage design files and tech packs in controlled storage or Git → track samples and supplier feedback via simple workflows → optionally graduate to a commercial fashion PLM as complexity grows. Suitable for startups and internal tool teams. Most established apparel brands rely on commercial fashion PLM for speed-to-market and industry-standard collaboration.
